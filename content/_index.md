In this course you design and build audio plugins: the effects and instruments
that live inside a DAW. We work in [plugdata](https://plugdata.org/), a visual
programming environment built on Pure Data that runs as a plugin in REAPER or
any other DAW. You connect boxes with cables, and the patch becomes a sound.
No coding or DSP background is expected.

Each week you build one of the standard plugins everyone uses: gain and pan,
tremolo, filters and EQ, delay, chorus and flanger, distortion, compression,
reverb, and a synthesizer. Along the way you learn how each one works on the
inside. Three times during the semester you design a plugin of your own and
export it with the Heavy compiler as a standalone VST3, AU, or CLAP that
anyone can load.
